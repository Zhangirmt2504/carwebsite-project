ABOUT THE PROJECT

Cars made simple

CAR is an educational front-end project that presents car categories in a clear and responsive website.

OUR IDEA

Why we created CAR

Choosing a car can be confusing because different body styles are designed for different needs. Our website organizes basic information into simple sections.

The project focuses on semantic HTML, modern CSS, Flexbox, Grid and responsive design.

“The right car is the one that fits your real needs.”

— CAR project idea
